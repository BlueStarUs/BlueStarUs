# Feeling pressure to know what your major will be?

Not sure if your resume is competitive for your dream major? Let us help! Our years of experience have given us a lot of insight… and one HUGE secret we've learned is this:

Admissions officers don't look for well-rounded students - they build well-rounded classes

Admissions committees want a diverse entering class of students with unique and focused interests .

Think about it - if you're building a team of superheroes to save the world, you don't choose team members with the same set of broad skills - you choose each hero for his or her incredibly focused and unique strength. That's what top colleges do!

So what's your take-away?
