# Introduction

Getting started on your personal statement? Before you write a single word, make sure you're not accidentally setting yourself up for stress later on.

At Blue Stars, we've seen that the most compelling essays start with strategy , not just inspiration. That's why we guide students through key decisions before the writing even begins-like how to interpret prompts, choose the right topic, and think about their overall story across all application materials.

Here are the most common pitfalls to avoid-and what to do instead- before you write:

# ON PROCESS - Prep & Planning

❌ DON'T
✅ DO

Skip reading the prompt (even if you think you are already familiar with it) before brainstorming.
Read each prompt carefully , marking it up to make sure you are answering the question(s) asked.

Decide on a topic for one essay without considering the full array of essays for each application.
Plan your topics strategically. Consider the themes of all your essays before deciding on a topic for your Common App Personal Statement.

Get too much help from others. Everyone will have different opinions about what you should write.
Stay true to your voice. Seek feedback, but ensure the final essay reflects your authentic self. A fresh perspective is often better than advice from those who know you too well.

Stress over whether your topic is "good enough."
Choose meaningful topics. What matters most is how you explore the topic, its impact on you, and what you learned.

Overanalyze "successful" essay examples.
Draw inspiration carefully. Focus on telling your own unique story in a way that is true to you.

Once you've laid the foundation, it's time to tackle the writing. But even strong starts can veer off course without careful attention to tone, detail, and structure. That's where Part 2 comes in.
