# #1: Work smarter, not necessarily harder - take time out to plan and organize

At Blue Stars we don't tell students to "just work harder!" That won't help. Figuring out how to maximize your study time will.

It can take a leap of faith to carve out time to plan and get organized. But this is a HUGE factor in students' success, both in school and for the rest of their lives.

Where do you start? Let's face it - sometimes it can be stressful just figuring out HOW to make yourself less stressed.

Blue Stars really feels for students, given all the expectations placed on them. We've devoted a lot of time to helping students stay organized and manage all the demands on their time.

# #3: Create a support system

Staying on track can be rough for all of us, even with the right tools.

That's why a great support system is important - people with experience to help you stay on track, set the right priorities, and solve tough time-management problems.

Get an "accountability partner" in your corner - a parent, adult mentor, experienced Blue Stars counselor - and set up regular times to meet. We suggest once or twice each month.

The key is to create accountability - get someone to help you come up with a plan to keep you calm, focused and confident.
