# What makes a college application essay stand out to admissions officers?

Admissions officers read thousands of essays. What stands out is authentic reflection and vulnerability rather than a recited resume. Top essays reveal how a student thinks, how they navigate setbacks, and what values guide their curiosity. Our founder, Dr. Amy Morgenstern, coaches students to locate their distinct narrative voice.

# How many drafts does a student typically write for their Common App essay?

On average, our students go through 4 to 6 thoughtful iterations. We begin with extensive ideation and free-writing, progress into structural outlining, and culminate in detailed line-editing and word-count polish. We never write for the student; we help them unlock their own finest literary voice.

# How do supplemental essays differ from the main personal statement?

While the personal statement is about WHO you are, supplemental essays usually answer 'Why THIS College?', 'Why THIS Major?', or ask about community contributions. They require deep school-specific research into specific professors, campus traditions, courses, and labs to prove genuine fit.

# Can Blue Stars help with the 8 UC Personal Insight Questions (PIQs)?

Absolutely. The UC system requires 4 essays of up to 350 words each. Because UCs don't conduct interviews or review teacher recommendations, PIQs are evaluated differently from Common App essays-they require clarity, context, and quantifiable impact. We have a dedicated UC PIQ curriculum.
