# What is an 'admissions spike' and is it better than being well-rounded?

Top universities no longer look for well-rounded students; they look for a well-rounded class comprised of singularly focused, angular students ('pointy' or 'spiky' profiles). A student who demonstrates deep, multi-year dedication to a focused intersection (e.g., computational biology and community health) stands out far more than someone who joins 10 unrelated clubs.

# Do prestigious paid summer programs help with college admissions?

Generally, no. Programs that cost thousands of dollars and admit anyone who can pay are viewed as 'pay-to-play' by admissions officers. Conversely, highly selective, free or merit-subsidized programs (like RSI, SSP, Clark Scholars, TASP, COSMOS) carry genuine admissions weight. We help students target truly competitive opportunities or design independent projects.

# How can high school students secure independent research with university professors?

Through targeted academic literature review and well-crafted outreach. We guide students on how to read professors' published papers, identify relevant research questions, and compose thoughtful cold emails that demonstrate genuine intellectual engagement.
