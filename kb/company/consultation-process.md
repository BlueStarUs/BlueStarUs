# Our process

01. Discover: Understand your goals, interests and strengths.
02. Plan: Create a personalized college list and strategy.
03. Prepare: Work on applications, essays and requirements.
04. Apply: Submit strong applications with confidence.

# How does the consultation process work?

You start by booking a complimentary 30-minute Initial Consultation so we can learn about your student's profile and family goals. This is followed by a 75-minute in-depth Strategy Consultation where we diagnose strengths, evaluate transcripts, and present an actionable college roadmap.

# Do you help with international college applications?

Yes. We guide students applying to colleges in different countries, including requirements, timelines and documentation.

# Do you help students find research opportunities and internships?

Yes! Our Research Academy and internship mentorship connects students with university laboratory positions (including Ivy League labs), faculty cold-email frameworks, and prestigious summer research fellowships.

# When should I start the college planning process?

The earlier the better. Ideally start 12 to 18 months before applications are due, but we support students at every stage.
