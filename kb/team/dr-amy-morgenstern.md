# Biography

Dr. Amy Morgenstern ("Dr. M") is the Founder of Blue Stars Admissions Consulting, established in 2010 to serve students and families navigating complex and competitive college admissions landscapes. A first-generation college student, Dr. M holds a Ph.D. in philosophy and an MFA in contemporary art. She is a former professor of philosophy, honors program associate director, and assistant to the director of an international academic and cultural studies program. Fluent in German, she served as a dissertation fellow in Germany for two years. As an award-winning writer, cultural theorist, and interdisciplinary artist, she brings deep institutional insight to selective admissions, combining structural analysis with careful narrative development so each student's application is coherent, intentional, and strategically positioned.
